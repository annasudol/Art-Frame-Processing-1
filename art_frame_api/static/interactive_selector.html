🖼️ Frame Selector

Click or drag corners for precision

📋 How to Use

Click 4 corners in order:

  1. Top-Left corner
  2. Top-Right corner
  3. Bottom-Right corner
  4. Bottom-Left corner
⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts:
  • S - Save coordinates
  • R - Reset selection
  • ESC - Clear selection
💡 Pro Tip: After placing all corners, you can click and drag any corner point to adjust its position for perfect alignment!
